# Three-page editorial review — Abstract, About, Contact

**Status:** Review baseline and decision record. Its 14 change recommendations were implemented in `content/pages/*.md` on 2026-09-30; the implementation checklist is in [the plan](PLAN-THREE-PAGE-EDITORIAL-REFINEMENT.md).

**Scope:** `content/pages/abstract.md`, `content/pages/about.md`, and `content/pages/contact.md`. The recommendation IDs below are stable within this document. Research supports the comprehension and interaction principles cited; the choice of professional emphasis remains an editorial judgment. The **Current copy** column quotes the pre-implementation source verbatim, including terminology not recommended for the **Reviewed copy** column.

## Strategic assessment

The three files tell a distinctive story: Abstract Voyage begins with curiosity, About shows a progression from building software to leading broader work, and Contact makes it easy to start a conversation. The strongest thread is the observation that plans change when people actually use what was built.

The main positioning gap is the connection between those facts. A first-time visitor has to infer that engineering leadership, interface engineering and development, and attention to human behavior are parts of one practice. A few passages also imply that deciding what to build has displaced building it. The recommendations below make that connection explicit while keeping engineering central and the voice understated.

## Final synthesis

**Current perception:** A thoughtful, technically capable person whose work has expanded from building software into larger decisions, but whose interface engineering experience and interest in human behavior require inference.

**Intended perception:** A senior engineer and leader whose software engineering, interface development, and product experience have converged around how people understand and use digital systems—and who remains close to implementation.

**Main positioning gap:** The existing story contains the ingredients, but often leaves the relationship between engineering decisions and human experience unstated.

**Narrative architecture:** Home identifies the person and the enduring question behind the work. About demonstrates how building, leading, and observing real use developed together. Contact invites either a technical or human-facing problem through an accurately described process.

**Highest-leverage recommendations:** HOME-01, HOME-02, ABOUT-03, ABOUT-04, ABOUT-05, ABOUT-06, CONTACT-03.
