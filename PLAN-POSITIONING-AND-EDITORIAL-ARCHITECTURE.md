# Positioning and editorial architecture plan

Status: **proposed for operator review**. This document turns the findings in [ANALYSIS-POSITIONING-AND-INFERENCE-AUDIT.md](ANALYSIS-POSITIONING-AND-INFERENCE-AUDIT.md) into discrete work items. It does not authorize or perform copy changes.

## Scope and decision rules

- Source corpus: every Markdown file in `content/pages/` and `posts/` as reviewed on 2026-09-30. The three page files are `abstract.md`, `about.md`, and `contact.md`; there are eleven posts.
- **Home-page recommendations are excluded.** Do not change `content/pages/abstract.md`, home presentation code, or `featured` selections under this plan. The home page appears below only where a post-metadata change could incidentally affect it.
- Preserve the original technical and historical character of the posts. Work only on the passages and metadata named here.
- Keep engineering current and foundational. Do not imply that implementation is delegated away or belongs only to an earlier career phase.
- Do not invent projects, responsibilities, client names, metrics, adoption effects, revenue effects, user counts, or research validation.
- Avoid occupational labels that split engineering from the broader work. Use concrete decisions, technical constraints, interface behavior, and documented leadership scope.
- Use `P1` for positioning-critical work, `P2` for meaningful improvement, and `P3` for optional refinement.
- Every proposed tag must be at most 18 characters including spaces. No measured keyword opportunity is claimed.

## Analysis to retain

### Current cumulative inference

The corpus reads as an experienced software engineer and team lead with broad technical interests and an exploratory interest in human interfaces. Engineering depth is well evidenced by the SOLID, DOM, compression, feature-toggle, technical-debt, and dependency articles. Leadership is evidenced by the two tech-lead articles and the About chronology. Human-system thinking is most explicit in the biomimetics article and in the About account of weighing use against build quality.

The main gap is connective tissue: technical decisions, interaction behavior, and senior judgment often appear as adjacent interests. Some featured older articles also make stronger technical or empirical claims than their own examples support. Those claims are especially costly to credibility because the site uses the articles as evidence of present judgment.

The corpus has **31 tag assignments and 29 distinct tags**. Only `Software Architecture` and `Software Engineering` recur. The existing vocabulary therefore does little to connect related work. The recommended taxonomy below has 13 terms; this is a semantic and editorial SEO hypothesis, not a verified keyword finding.

### Intended inference

A senior software engineer and technical leader who can shape digital systems through implementation judgment and close attention to how people understand and use them. The broader specialization should be inferable from examples before any explicit label is needed.

### Content roles

| Surface | Role | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| About | Show the progression from building software to leading teams and carrying technical decisions through delivery. | Keep scope bounded to what the page documents. |
| Contact | Invite a clear description of a technical, product, or interaction problem; preserve control over the note sent. | Set only expectations the process can meet. |
| Engineering posts | Demonstrate technical fundamentals, architecture, frontend behavior, delivery, and tradeoff judgment. | Correct inaccuracies without making historical articles sound newly published. |
| Leadership posts | Show how decisions, stakeholder context, and coordination are shared. | Keep leadership grounded in technical work. |
| Interface post | Show serious curiosity about human-system interaction. | Present the framework as exploratory until its claims are evidenced. |
| Education post and Welcome | Supply supporting context and history. | Do not let a second biography or broad interests define the current profile. |

### Evidence standard

Recommendations labeled **theory informed** apply general models to this site; those models did not test this copy. **Strategic/editorial judgment** covers accuracy, positioning, voice, and taxonomy choices. None is labeled **empirically informed**, because no visitor study tested these specific pages. Technical corrections must be checked against authoritative technical sources or by a qualified reviewer before publication. See [References](#references).

## Implementation sequence

1. **Accuracy before prominence.** Resolve the technical and empirical claims in the featured SOLID, dependencies, and biomimetics articles. Do not add links that promote an article while its central claim remains unresolved.
2. **Core page continuity.** Make the bounded About changes, then review Contact expectations. Keep the About closing statement that decisions are carried through implementation.
3. **Article discovery copy.** Update only the three titles and four excerpts in the tables below after their related body changes settle. Preserve all other titles and excerpts.
4. **Taxonomy and links.** Apply the post tag map and the small set of genuinely relevant internal links. Check the incidental effect of first-tag changes on existing home spotlight labels before accepting the metadata edits.
5. **Final review.** Check factual support, technical accuracy, title/excerpt fidelity, tag length, link destinations, and tone. Review the rendered affected pages and post listings if implementation changes visible copy or metadata, following `AGENTS.md` resource-safety and visual-verification rules.
